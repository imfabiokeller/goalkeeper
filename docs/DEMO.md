# The demo

Three minutes, then Q&A. No slides. Already running when the slot starts.
Maya owns this file.

## Script

**0:00 to 0:30.** QR on screen. "Scan and you can add to the work." Point
at the goal in the header: "a human approved this, nothing else was
written by a human today." Point at the counters: "hundreds of millions of
tokens through the library since 12:30; every request a worker saw was
under 20k."

**0:30 to 1:00.** Kill five workers on screen (`docker compose kill`).
Their rows go red, their cells fall back to grey, other rows pick them up.
"Workers are disposable. The memory is not." Open one task page: the
context it was given, the passages retrieved from the library, the
proposal, the gate.

**1:00 to 2:15.** The live round. Crowd requests land as cards and move to
their outcome within seconds: a unit from the reserve gets queued and
lights up, a recheck runs, a guideline becomes a proposal in the inbox, a
"rank the greenest" gets parked with its reason. Approve the proposal on
the phone: goal version ticks to 2, the red blocked cells go grey, blue,
green.

**2:15 to 2:45.** The timeline: units done climbing, first-attempt pass
rate rising as the library fills, blocked count dropping at the version
marker. "Every merge passed a deterministic check against a criterion a
human approved."

**2:45 to 3:00.** "Library and goal. Agents own the big part that never
needs reading; humans own the small part that matters." Stop.

## The one-minute video

Same script cut to: the kill, one task page with its sources, one crowd
request shipping, the approval wave, the counters. Recorded by 16:30 from a
real run.

## Moments to rehearse until they cannot fail

- Kill-and-resume.
- A task page opened with its retrieved sources.
- One crowd request classified and merged inside 90 seconds (use a
  prepared one if the room is slow).
- The parked request with its reason.
- Approve on the phone, blocked cells resolve.
