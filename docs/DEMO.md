# The demo

Three minutes, then one to two minutes of Q&A (three in the final). No
slides. Already running when the slot starts.

## Script

**0:00 to 0:30.** QR on screen. "Scan and you can add to the goal." Point
at the lens: "a human wrote this, nothing else was written by a human
today." Point at the counters: "600 million tokens through the library
since 10:30; every request the workers saw was about 15k."

**0:30 to 1:00.** Kill five workers on screen. The queue keeps draining.
"Workers are disposable. The memory is not. A new worker's briefing comes
from the raw record, with the sources attached." Open one briefing, show
the passages behind it.

**1:00 to 2:15.** The live round. Crowd requests land, get classified, one
becomes a task with a test written first, workers claim, the gate passes
and fails, changes ship. One request gets parked with a reason on screen.

**2:15 to 2:45.** The ledger: files migrated, features shipped, reverts by
the drift check. "Every merge is tied to a criterion. The drift check
reverted two that were not."

**2:45 to 3:00.** "Library and lens. Agents own the big part that never
needs reading; humans own the small part that matters." Stop.

## The one-minute video

Same script cut to: the kill, one briefing with its sources, one crowd
request shipping, the counters. Recorded by 16:30 from a real run.

## Moments to rehearse until they cannot fail

- Kill-and-resume.
- A briefing opened with its sources.
- One request classified and shipped inside 90 seconds (use a prepared
  one if the room is slow).
- The parked request with its reason.
